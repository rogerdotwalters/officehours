    How the day works

    Most of you are productive employees. A secret few are slackers.

    Everyone gets the same tasks, one at a time through the day, but each role does its own version. Productive employees heat their lunch, chart the quarterly results on the whiteboard and restock the toilet paper. Slackers microwave fish for 15 minutes, doodle BOSS SUCKS and clog the toilet.

    Productive work fills the Productivity meter; shenanigans fill the Chaos meter. Shenanigans leave messes behind, like fish fumes, rude doodles and floods, and a productive employee doing their task at the same spot cleans the mess up.

    Catch the slackers: ring the bell in the Conference Room for an all-hands meeting and vote them out, or report them at the HR box in the Lobby (if you're wrong, you're fired). Walls block your view, so watch the doorways.

    Productive employees win when Productivity hits 100% or every slacker is fired. Slackers win when Chaos hits 100% or there are as many slackers as productive employees. At 5 PM, the higher meter wins.
