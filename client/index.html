    How the day works

    One of you is secretly Management, maybe helped by a few secret snitches. Everyone else is a worker.

    Tasks arrive one at a time as the workday goes on. Finish them all, then clock out at the time clock in the Lobby. If enough workers clock out before five o'clock, workers win.

    Management sends people home by catching them away from their desks, or by calling a desk check: when the countdown ends, anyone not at their own desk goes home.

    You can only see what's in your line of sight. Walls block your view, so watch the corners.

    Out on the floor nobody talks: you can only emote, and only people who can see you notice. To talk, sit at your own desk and open your terminal. It has #general for everyone, the water cooler for workers and the back office for Management. Snitches work like everyone else but sit in both private channels, passing on what they hear, and win with Management.

    The breaker box at the end of the west hallway kills the wifi. With no wifi, nobody can be reported or desk-checked, but terminals are offline too. Errands finished during an outage fill the team's social meter, and a full meter wins the day for the workers.

    The day has breaks. On a break you can't be reported in the Break Room or anywhere outside, Management can't call desk checks, and lunch tasks can finally be done.

    Anyone can ring the bell in the Conference Room once to call an all-hands meeting and vote someone out.
